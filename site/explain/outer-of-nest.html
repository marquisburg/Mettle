outer-of-nestThe outer loop of a nest, reported for context

Reported by --explain after a verdict.

mettle explain outer-of-nest

Recorded so the report accounts for every loop rather than going quiet about the outer one. Only innermost loops vectorize. The verdict that matters is on the inner loop's own remark.
