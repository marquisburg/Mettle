rewrite-rule-unusedA `rewrite` rule matched nothing

Reported by --explain after a verdict.

mettle explain rewrite-rule-unused

No expression had the shape of the rule's from side at any point the compiler looked: before inlining, when calls are still calls, and after it, when arithmetic has been folded and propagated. The rule costs nothing, but it is doing nothing.

The usual causes: the rule's parameter types differ from the code's (a rule over int64 does not match int32 arithmetic), or the compiler's own folding reached the expression first.
