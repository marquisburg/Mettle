rewrite-appliedAn expression was rewritten by a `rewrite` rule

Reported by --explain after a verdict.

mettle explain rewrite-applied

The expression had the shape of a rule's from side, the rule's where guard (if any) held on the constants at this site, and the expression became the rule's to side. The function was then executed before and after the change on generated inputs, and only a match in every observation lets the rewrite stand.
